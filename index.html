IRA

An assistant that handles your Gmail, Drive and Calendar from a single sentence.

LangGraphLangChainMCPRAGFastAPIGoogle APIs View code ↗

An orchestrator built on LangGraph reads the request, breaks it into steps, and hands each step to a Gmail, Drive or Calendar agent. The agents act through MCP tools served from a FastAPI backend, and a retrieval layer helps them work out things like which deck you meant.

    Computer vision · 2026

    Gesture OS

    Control your computer with your hand: no mouse, no touch.

    PythonOpenCVMediaPipePyAutoGUI View code ↗

    MediaPipe finds 21 landmarks on your hand in every webcam frame. Gesture OS works out which fingers are up and how far apart they are, turns that into a gesture, and fires the matching action through PyAutoGUI: moving the mouse, clicking, scrolling, changing the volume, taking screenshots and triggering app shortcuts.

    The hard part is making it feel right. A cursor that lags or jitters is worse than no cursor, so most of the tuning went into low-latency, stable detection.

    Resume Analyzer

    An ATS-style checker that tells you why you got your score.

    PythonLangChainRAGGemini API GitHub ↗

    Built during placement season to answer the question everyone in my batch was asking: how does my resume actually compare to this job? It embeds the resume and the job description, matches them by meaning with RAG, and produces an ATS-style score, a list of skill gaps, and resume and interview suggestions from Gemini.
